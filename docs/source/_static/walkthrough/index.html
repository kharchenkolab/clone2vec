Part 2 · Baseline

Compare clones directly: E-distance

E-distancebetween every pair of clones

Before clone2vec, the direct way to compare two clones is the energy distance between their cell distributions in expression space: E(A,B) = 2·mean‖a−b‖ − mean‖a−a′‖ − mean‖b−b′‖. It is zero when the two clones are drawn from the same distribution. It needs no neighbor graph, but it only sees overlap of cells: two clones that sit in the same structure without sharing positions still look far apart, and small clones give noisy estimates.

clone2vec: describe clones by who they co-occur with
